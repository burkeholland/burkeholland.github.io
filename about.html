About Me

Hey, I'm Burke. I'm a web developer in Nashville, TN who spent years telling people about JavaScript frameworks and now spends most of his time talking to an AI in a text box. Career progression is a funny thing.

I got into AI the way most people get into things they won't shut up about -- I tried it once, it worked unreasonably well, and now it's my whole personality. I've been a developer for a long time, and nothing has changed how I build things as much as AI has. I'm not saying that to be dramatic. I'm saying it because I mass-produced an entire project last Tuesday that would have taken me a month, and honestly I'm still a little shook about it.

This blog is where I write about what I'm learning, what I'm building, and what I'm getting wrong -- which is a lot. I don't have all the answers. I barely have some of them. But I figure things out in public because that's more interesting than pretending I already know everything. See what I've been building lately.
