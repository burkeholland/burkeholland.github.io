About

Burke Holland is a developer living in Nashville, TN — the greatest city in the world, probably. He works with the Copilot CLI team at Microsoft, where he spends most of his time thinking about AI-powered development and trying to figure out if any of it actually works.

Burke blogs only slightly better than he codes, but his YouTube channel at youtube.com/burkeholland is where most of the real damage gets done. He approaches AI not as someone who believes the hype, but as someone who has been burned by it enough times to have opinions.

You can find him on X as @burkeholland.
